Delete your VibeFeed account

Last updated 27 July 2026

VibeFeed is developed by Deniz Filikci. You can permanently delete your VibeFeed account at any time, directly inside the app. You do not need to contact us to do it.

How to delete your account in the app

  1. Go to your profile (the tab on the far right).
  2. Tap Edit profile.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Type SLET to confirm, then tap Permanently delete my account.

The confirmation word is SLET (Danish for "delete") in every language, and the app tells you so on the screen.

What gets deleted

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. We delete your account and everything that belongs to it:

  • your profile, your username, your profile picture and your banner
  • all your posts, thoughts, memories, stories, comments, likes and poll votes
  • your messages and the images you sent in chat
  • your friendships, circle memberships, invitations and requests
  • the files you uploaded to our storage
  • your login and your device's notification token

What is kept, and for how long

Nothing is kept after deletion. There is no retention period and no grace period. Once the deletion has gone through, your data no longer exists in our database, and it disappears from our backups as those backups rotate.

Deleting individual items without deleting your account

You do not have to delete your whole account to remove your data. Inside the app you can delete each of the following yourself, at any time, and it is removed permanently:

  • individual posts, both thoughts and memories, from the ⋯ menu on the post
  • individual comments, from the ⋯ menu on the comment
  • your stories, from the ⋯ menu while viewing your own story
  • individual chat messages, from the menu on the message
  • your likes, your reactions on messages and your saved posts, by tapping them again

Leaving a circle also removes you from it, and unfriending removes the connection for both people.

If you cannot access the app

If you cannot log in, or you no longer have the app installed, you can ask us to delete the account manually. Write to official@vibefeed.dk from the email address the account was created with, and we will delete it and confirm by email.

Unconfirmed accounts

An account that is created but never confirmed by email is deleted automatically after 24 hours.

Contact

Questions about deletion or about your data: official@vibefeed.dk
